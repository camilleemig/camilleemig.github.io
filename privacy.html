Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

The short version: ddupe does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Your photo library

ddupe asks for access to your photo library so it can find similar photos. It analyzes your photos entirely on your device. Your photos, and any information derived from them, are never uploaded, sent to a server, or shared with anyone — including the developer.

You can change or revoke ddupe's access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Data stored on your device

To avoid re-scanning your whole library every time, ddupe saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • Compact numerical "fingerprints" of your photos, used to compare them for similarity
  • Scan results and progress, so an interrupted scan can resume
  • Your settings and the groups you've reviewed or hidden

This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Clear Cache in ddupe's Settings, and it is removed completely when you delete the app.

Deleting and changing photos

ddupe only deletes or changes photos (for example, converting a Live Photo to a still) when you ask it to. iOS asks you to confirm each change, and deleted photos are moved to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for up to 30 days.

No tracking, analytics, or ads

ddupe has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. It does not track your activity in the app or across other apps and websites, and it does not make network connections.

Children

Because ddupe collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
