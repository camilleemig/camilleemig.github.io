How it works

Grouped by similarity.

ddupe looks at what's actually in each photo, gathers the near-identical ones into a group, and lets you decide what stays.

  1. Scan your library

    ddupe works through your photos on your iPhone and surfaces groups as it finds them — no waiting for the whole scan to finish. Put it down anytime; it picks up where it left off.

  2. Pick the keeper

    Flip through each group side by side. ddupe suggests which ones to let go, and you have the final word on every photo.

  3. Delete the rest

    Confirm and the extras are gone. iOS asks before anything is removed, and deleted photos wait in Recently Deleted in case you change your mind.

Features

Built for big libraries.

Finds near-duplicates, not just exact copies

Bursts, retakes, slightly different angles, the same scene a second later — ddupe recognizes photos that look alike even when the files are different.

Adjustable sensitivity

Strict to catch only near-identical shots, relaxed to catch more variations — or set your own cutoff anywhere from 50% to 100%.

Slim down Live Photos

Turn Live Photos you don't need into stills, choosing the exact frame to keep. Dates, locations, and other details come along.

Your whole library, or one album

Scan everything at once, focus on a single album, or browse your library by year, month, or week to tackle it a piece at a time.

Privacy

Always private.

ddupe needs access to your photo library to look for duplicates — and that's where your photos stay. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Support

Questions & answers.

Will ddupe delete photos on its own?

No. ddupe only suggests what to remove. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, iOS asks for permission before every deletion, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Never. All analysis happens on your iPhone. ddupe doesn't connect to any server and doesn't collect any data about you or your library.

How long does a scan take?

It depends on the size of your library and your iPhone. Groups start appearing right away, so you can begin reviewing while the scan continues. If you leave the app, the scan resumes where it stopped next time.

ddupe grouped photos that aren't really duplicates.

Open Settings in ddupe and choose a stricter scan sensitivity. Stricter levels only group photos that are near-identical; relaxed levels catch more variations with more false matches. You can also hide a group so it doesn't come back.

Does ddupe work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. ddupe works with the library in the Photos app, including photos stored in iCloud. Photos you delete are removed from all your devices that use iCloud Photos, just like deleting in the Photos app.

I only gave ddupe access to some photos.

ddupe can only see the photos you've shared with it. To let it scan your whole library, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › ddupe and choose Full Access.

How do I start over?

In ddupe's Settings, tap Clear Cache. This discards saved scan results so the next scan starts fresh. Your photos are not affected.
